Typography and Text Styling Demo

Typography plays a crucial role in web design, influencing how users read and perceive content. Proper use of fonts, sizes, and spacing enhances readability and visual appeal.

Different font families and weights can be combined to create contrast and hierarchy. Designers often mix sans-serif for headings with serif fonts for body text to balance modern and classic aesthetics.

Adjusting line-height and letter-spacing improves legibility, especially on screens. Aligning text properly and highlighting key points using mark, strong, and em tags ensures clarity and focus.
